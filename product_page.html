What is it?

Nose-Opener is a small, soft clip that rests on your nose while you sleep. It gently holds your nostrils open so air can flow in more easily, helping you breathe through your nose all night instead of your mouth.

  • Opens the nasal airways for easier breathing
  • Helps reduce snoring and mouth breathing

How it works

One clip. Wider airways.

Your nostrils are the narrowest part of your nose. When they close up at night, less air gets through. Nose-Opener keeps them open in three simple steps.

01

Place it

Before bed, slide the two soft tips just inside your nostrils. The band rests under the tip of your nose.

02

It opens

The flexible band gently pushes the sides of your nostrils outward, making the opening wider.

03

Breathe easy

More air flows through your nose, so you are less likely to breathe through your mouth or snore.
